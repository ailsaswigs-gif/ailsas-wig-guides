# Human Hair Wig Guide

## What Is a Human Hair Wig?

Human hair wigs are made from real human hair and provide the most natural appearance available. They can be washed, styled, curled, and straightened just like natural hair.

## Benefits of Human Hair Wigs

- Natural appearance
- Longer lifespan
- Heat styling flexibility
- Comfortable wear
- Better movement

## Human Hair Wig vs Synthetic Wig

Human hair wigs generally offer superior realism and durability compared to synthetic alternatives.

## Choosing the Right Wig Density

Popular options include:

- 130% Density
- 150% Density
- 180% Density

## Popular Wig Textures

- Straight
- Body Wave
- Deep Wave
- Water Wave
- Curly

## Wig Care Tips

To extend the lifespan of your wig:

1. Wash gently.
2. Use sulfate-free products.
3. Air dry whenever possible.
4. Store on a wig stand.
